# Payroll Endpoints

- **HTTP Method & URL**: `POST /api/v1/payroll/structures`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/structures`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/structures/:id`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `POST /api/v1/payroll/cycles`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/cycles`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/cycles/:id`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `PATCH /api/v1/payroll/cycles/:id/status`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `POST /api/v1/payroll/runs`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/runs`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/runs/:id`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `POST /api/v1/payroll/runs/:id/lock`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `POST /api/v1/payroll/runs/:id/finalize-payslips`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/payslips/my`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/payslips/:id`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/payslips/run/:runId`
- **HTTP Method & URL**: `GET /api/v1/payroll/payslips/employee/:employeeId`
